About

I'm a Lead AI Engineer at Tesana, where I co-architected the agentic engine that turns a text prompt into a playable 3D game. The engine was demoed live at GDC 2026, and the platform has reached about 400k users.

I build agentic systems end to end: fine-tuning and evaluating models, optimizing inference to run on our own hardware, and designing the harnesses agents act inside. I also built real-time multimodal agents that play games from screenshots alone.

I'm self-taught. I ran a restaurant kitchen until April 2025 and was leading AI engineering less than a year later. What pulls me forward now is understanding how these models actually work inside: mechanistic interpretability, alignment, and AI security.
