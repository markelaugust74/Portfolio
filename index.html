About

I'm a Lead AI Engineer at Tesana AI, building systems that turn natural language into playable 3D games. I work on the Muranyi and Atomos game models, compiler pipelines, agentic workflows, and the glue between language models and real-time runtimes.

Before Tesana, I built media restoration and computer-vision tooling at Echo, and a run of solo products around local inference — desktop wrappers for ComfyUI, Live2D chat clients, and open MCP servers.

I care about local-first AI, fast iteration, and shipping things people can actually use. Day to day that means a lot of llama.cpp, vLLM, and squeezing models onto real hardware.
